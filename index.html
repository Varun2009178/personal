geospatial foundation models for flood segmentation

independent study testing whether frozen-encoder geospatial foundation models (prithvi-eo-2.0, clay) beat a fully trained u-net when labels are scarce and the test region is unseen. uses sen1floods11 in a 90-run matrix — 3 models × 5 label budgets × 3 seeds — with held-out events in somalia, sri lanka, and spain.

+0.21
water iou, clay vs. u-net (somalia, 10% labels)

key finding: at 10% labels, clay beat the u-net on every unseen event across all seeds — foundation models are not uniformly superior. accepted as a poster at agu26 (december 2026).
